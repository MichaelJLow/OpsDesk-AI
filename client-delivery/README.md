# Client Delivery Toolkit

These materials translate the **synthetic OpsDesk / Quayside** demonstration into a **repeatable method** for discovering, piloting and handing over a similar system inside a real company.

They are **templates and methodology**, not evidence of completed client engagements. Do not present them as case-study results.

## Relationship to OpsDesk AI

OpsDesk proves engineering in a controlled sandbox. This folder proves **how you would engage** a real operations team without copying synthetic data or credentials blindly.
